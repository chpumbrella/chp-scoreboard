# How the pool works

Everyone drafts 10 teams. Your teams' wins and losses are your record. A win only counts if it comes against a Division I opponent; every loss counts.

## Weekly prizes

Generally 45–50% of the prize pool. Weekly records include all 10 of your teams.

- Best record: $35
- Second: $25
- Third: $15
- Fourth: $10

Ties are not broken, and players with the same winning percentage are tied (12–6 ties 10–5). Tied players split the combined money for the places they occupy, so two players tied for 4th each get $5, and two tied for 3rd each get $12.50.

After the new year, weeks run Monday through Sunday. Before the new year, some weeks may be combined. Target is 15–16 weeks.

## Season standings

Generally 20–25% of the prize pool. The first half and second half are scored separately. In each half, your worst team is dropped and your other 9 teams count. Your season record is your first-half top 9 plus your second-half top 9.

1st $150, 2nd $125, 3rd $100, 4th $80, 5th $70, 6th $60, 7th $50, 8th $40, 9th $30, 10th $20.

## Redraft

There is a redraft the week of January 5, with one team dropped (your choice) and a draft to add a team. Only teams available at the main draft are available, and you can't draft a team you already own. Order is based on a lottery, weighted to favor teams lower in the standings entering the redraft. The second half begins the following week.

## NCAA tournament

Generally 15% of the prize pool. Prizes are based on the furthest round your team reaches. Redrafted teams only count for the Final Four or better, and receive half the listed prize. If a team no one drafted makes the Sweet 16 or beyond, that payout goes to whoever finishes last in the full-year standings.

- Champion: $36
- Runner-up: $24
- Final Four loser: $18
- Elite 8 loser: $12
- Sweet 16 loser: $6

## Miscellaneous season-long prizes

Generally 10% of the prize pool. These include full-season teams only (not the team you drop at the redraft or the team you add).

- Most teams in the NCAA tournament among the 9 teams you hold all year: $16 / $8
- Highest average seed of your teams that make the tournament: $16 / $8
- Worst overall record of any team taken in the pool and held all year: $16 / $8
- NIT champion and runner-up: $24 / $12 (if unowned, split among the NIT champ or runner-up; if both unowned, last place gets it)
- Worst record in any individual week: $16 / $8
- Best worst team: $16 / $8
- Worst best team: $16 / $8
- Best 10th-round pick: $16 / $8
- Napoleon Dynamite, a guillotine pool that eliminates 3 per week: $40 / $10 / $10 / $10
- Hebert Cup, head-to-head matchups weeks 2–6: $40 / $20 / $10 / $10

Hebert Cup tiebreakers: number of wins, then prior week win %, then point differential, then win % of your #10 pick, followed by #9, and so on.

## Tournament prizes

$5 to the winner of several key Thanksgiving tournaments, such as the Charleston Classic, Maui Invitational, Battle 4 Atlantis, Legends Classic, Bahamas Championship, Greenbrier Tipoff, Sunshine Slam, ESPN Events Invitational, NIT Season Tip-Off, Arizona Tipoff, Rady Children’s Invitational and Acrisure Classic.
